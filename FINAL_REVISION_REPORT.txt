FACT-SAFETY RULE
The theme intentionally does not invent sourcing countries, suppliers, auctions, import/customs processes, delivery regions, guaranteed lead times, minimum order values, fixed prices, company history, physical address or business hours. Those details should be entered only after client confirmation.

REFERENCE NOTE
